### SLIDE 3: Brief About the Idea
* **Title:** Brief About the Idea — What is Tripbone?
* **Sub-Heading:** An AI-Native Tour Commerce & Autonomous Operating System built 100% on Google Cloud.
* **Core Idea & Value Proposition:**
  * **The Platform:** Tripbone is a multi-tenant SaaS that empowers independent tour and activity operators to launch luxury, high-converting direct-booking storefronts and automate their entire business operations using Google Cloud.
  * **Autonomous AI Workforce:** Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, Tripbone provides an intelligent AI Tour Builder, a catalog-grounded AI Travel Planner, a 24/7 AI Sales Concierge, and an AI Email Booking Extractor.
  * **Universal BYOPG (0% Commission):** With a Bring-Your-Own-Payment-Gateway architecture (Stripe, Midtrans QRIS, PayPal), operators collect 100% of customer payments directly into their own merchant bank accounts with zero platform take-rate.
  * **Target Audience:** Over 2.5 million independent tour operators, day-cruise operators, trekking guides, and cultural activity providers across Southeast Asia and global travel destinations.

### SLIDE 4: Opportunities
* **Header:** Opportunities & Market Differentiation
* **Bullet 1: How different is it from existing ideas?**
  * *Legacy Booking Engines (Bókun, FareHarbor, Rezdy):* Complex, clunky legacy interfaces, expensive (charging 2%–6% per booking + credit card fees), with zero native Generative AI capabilities.
  * *Generic E-Commerce (Shopify, Wix, WordPress):* Lack travel-specific logistics (departure time slots, hotel pickup zones, dynamic group pricing tiers, guide/driver WhatsApp dispatch, and interactive multi-day itineraries).
  * *Tripbone Difference:* The first unified, mobile-first platform combining an Airbnb-grade direct storefront + autonomous AI operations engine + 0% platform commission.
* **Bullet 2: How will it be able to solve the problem?**
  * Reduces catalog creation time from 1 hour to 2 seconds using Gemini 2.5.
  * Captures international travelers 24/7 via an AI Concierge that answers hotel pickup and equipment questions instantly in multiple languages.
  * Eliminates manual WhatsApp booking retyping by extracting unstructured partner emails into verified bookings with scannable QR vouchers.
  * Removes the 25% OTA middleman tax, allowing operators to increase net profit margins by 20–30%.
* **Bullet 3: Unique Selling Proposition (USP):**
  1. **Catalog-Grounded Gemini Itinerary Planning:** The AI Trip Planner doesn't just output generic text—it dynamically embeds the operator's actual active tours with 1-click booking drawers.
  2. **Zero-Data-Entry AI Dispatch:** Automatic conversion of raw email inquiries into verified reservations and digital QR check-in tickets.
  3. **Universal BYOPG Model:** 0% commission, direct local & multi-currency settlement.

### SLIDE 9: Technologies Used in the Solution
* **Header:** Technology Stack Summary
* **Google Cloud Technologies (Core):**
  * **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API:** High-speed, multimodal Gen AI engine for streaming trip planning, natural language tour generation, and structured email extraction.
  * **Google Cloud Run:** Fully managed serverless container runtime hosting the production Node.js backend with automated zero-to-N scaling.
  * **Firebase Firestore:** Enterprise NoSQL cloud database providing sub-80ms real-time multi-tenant data synchronization and audit logging.
  * **Firebase Authentication:** Multi-role authentication (Admin, Operator Staff, Driver, Guest) with JWT validation.
  * **Google Maps Platform:** Interactive map rendering, geocoding, and transit route optimization.
* **Frontend & UI Libraries:**
  * React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React, Canvas Confetti.
* **Payments & Integrations:**
  * Universal BYOPG Layer: Stripe Elements, Midtrans Snap/QRIS, PayPal SDK, Xendit.
